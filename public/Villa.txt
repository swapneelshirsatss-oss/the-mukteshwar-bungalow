6. LOCAL ATTRACTIONS & DISTANCE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Kumaon Destinations & Sightseeing from The Mukteshwar Bungalow:

1. Chauli Ki Jali (8.5 km | ~20 min drive)
   - Famous cliffside rock formation with 360-degree valley views and sunset panoramas.
2. Mukteshwar Dham Shiva Temple (8.2 km | ~20 min drive)
   - 350-year-old historic stone Shiva shrine perched at 2,286 meters altitude.
3. Bhalu Gaad Waterfall (14.0 km | ~30 min drive + gentle scenic forest trek)
   - 60-foot pristine cascading natural waterfall and crystal swimming pool.
4. Kasialekh Fruit Orchards & Ridge Trails (0.5 km | Walking distance)
   - Strolling through local apple, plum, and peach orchards in season.
5. Ramgarh Valley (18 km | ~35 min drive)
   - Renowned fruit bowl of Kumaon with historic colonial ties.
6. Kainchi Dham Neem Karoli Baba Ashram (32 km | ~1 hr 10 min drive)
   - Revering spiritual pilgrimage shrine in the Kumaon hills.
7. Nainital Town & Naini Lake (42 km | ~1.5 hr drive)
   - Premier lake district hub with boating, Mall Road, and cable car.
