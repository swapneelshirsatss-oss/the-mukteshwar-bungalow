- **Brand Accent (10%)**: Himalayan Red (`#7B1E2D`) — Direct reservation buttons, active nav, logo accent.
- **Typography**: Display: `Cormorant Garamond` (Luxury serif) | Body: `Manrope` (Modern sans-serif).
